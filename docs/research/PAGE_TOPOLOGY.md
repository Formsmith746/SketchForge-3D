# Tinkercad Landing Page Topology

Target: `https://www.tinkercad.com/` (landing page only).

1. Fixed blue navigation, 81px desktop / 60px mobile.
2. 500px desktop / 375px mobile video hero.
3. Overlapping feature-video poster and centered introductory copy/actions.
4. Three trust cards; illustrations are hidden on mobile.
5. Blue 100-million-user banner.
6. Three product rows with text left/video right; image-first stacked cards on mobile.
7. Light-gray educational standards split feature.
8. Curriculum heading plus four-card desktop grid; first three cards on mobile.
9. Blue Autodesk call-to-action banner.
10. Five-column footer, privacy bands, and legal links.

The document scrolls natively. The header is fixed and does not change state while scrolling.
